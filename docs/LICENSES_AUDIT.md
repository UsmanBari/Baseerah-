## Namaz recitations (data/namaz/recitations.json)
- Quran passages (Bismillah, Al-Fatihah, Al-Ikhlas, Ibrahim 14:40-41) are copied at build time from data/surahs by `scripts/build_namaz_recitations.py` (Tanzil Arabic; Junagarhi Urdu; Saheeh International English via QuranEnc). `tests/test_kiosk.py` checks they match exactly.
- Sunnah adhkar (Takbeer, Thana, Ta'awwudh, Ruku/Sajdah tasbeeh, Tasmee/Tahmeed, Jalsa, Tashahhud, Durood Ibrahimi, Salam) use the standard Hanafi wording with hadith references; Urdu/English meanings were prepared for Baseerah. Recorded as `reviewed_by: pending-scholar-review` until a scholar signs off.

## Added Nov 2026
- **qrcode-generator 1.4.4** (Kazuhiko Arase, MIT) vendored as `js/vendor/qrcode.esm.js` for "Continue on your phone" QR codes and the QR on shared ayah images. "QR Code" is a registered trademark of DENSO WAVE INCORPORATED.
- **Word statistics** (`data/word_freq.js`, `data/vocab.js`) are computed by `scripts/build_word_data.py` from the app's own word-by-word data; no new third-party text.
- **Notification chime** is synthesised with Web Audio in `js/core/notifications.js` (no audio file).
